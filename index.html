MotoGP 2026: Aragon Grand Prix - Event Information

About Aragon Grand Prix

The Aragon GP represents a landmark moment in the 2026 MotoGP season at MotorLand Aragón. This edition holds special significance as the premier class races with 1000cc engines for the final year before new regulations introduce 850cc machines from 2027. The Spanish circuit in Alcañiz, Teruel province, hosts all three Grand Prix classes alongside the introduction of the Harley-Davidson Bagger World Cup to select rounds across Europe and North America.

The MotorLand Aragon Circuit

MotorLand Aragón stretches 5.077 kilometers across the Aragon landscape. The circuit features 17 turns, with 10 left corners and 7 right corners. The longest straight extends 968 meters. German architect Hermann Tilke designed the track with British firm Foster and Partners, while former Formula 1 driver Pedro de la Rosa provided technical input.
The facility stands out as one of five circuits on the MotoGP calendar that runs counter-clockwise. The track presents dramatic elevation changes, with 50 meters difference between its highest and lowest points. This combination of long straights, hard braking zones, and technical corners creates a versatile racing environment. The venue accommodates 129,500 spectators.

MotoGP 2026: Aragon GP - More Information

Race Weekend Format

The Aragon GP weekend includes multiple sessions across MotoGP, Moto2, and Moto3 classes. Free Practice and Practice sessions allow riders to learn the circuit and refine their setups. Qualifying determines grid positions through Q1 and Q2 sessions.

Saturday features the Tissot Sprint race, which runs half the distance of the main Grand Prix. The top nine finishers score points, from 12 points for first place down to 1 point for ninth. Sunday's main Grand Prix awards points to the top fifteen riders, with 25 points for the winner descending to 1 point for fifteenth place.

Circuit History

Street races ran through Alcañiz from 1963 to 2003 before safety concerns prompted the creation of a purpose-built facility. MotorLand Aragón opened in 2009 and joined the MotoGP World Championship calendar in 2010, replacing the Hungarian Grand Prix. The circuit won the IRTA Best Grand Prix of the Year award in its debut season, a first for a new venue. The circuit has received this honor three times total.

The complex includes three zones: a technology park for motor industry research and education, a sports area with the main circuit and karting track, and a leisure and culture area. The facility also hosts World Superbike Championship and European Le Mans Series events.

Expected Participants

The provisional 2026 MotoGP grid features established champions and rising stars. The Ducati Lenovo Team pairs Marc Marquez with Francesco Bagnaia. Monster Energy Yamaha MotoGP fields Fabio Quartararo and Alex Rins. Red Bull KTM Factory Racing runs Pedro Acosta alongside Brad Binder. Repsol Honda Team includes Joan Mir and Luca Marini. Aprilia Racing combines Jorge Martin with Marco Bezzecchi.

Satellite teams add depth to the field. Pertamina Enduro VR46 Ducati features Fabio Di Giannantonio and Franco Morbidelli. Gresini Racing Ducati runs Alex Marquez with Fermin Aldeguer. Prima Pramac Racing switches to Yamaha machinery for Toprak Razgatlioglu and Jack Miller. Trackhouse Aprilia fields Raul Fernandez and Ai Ogura. LCR Honda pairs Johann Zarco with Diogo Moreira. Red Bull KTM Tech3 combines Maverick Viñales and Enea Bastianini.

Three-time World Superbike Champion Toprak Razgatlioglu makes his Grand Prix debut with Prima Pramac Yamaha. Reigning Moto2 World Champion Diogo Moreira steps up to the premier class with Pro Honda LCR.
